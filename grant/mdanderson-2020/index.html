A Mechanistic Tumor Growth Model for Hyperpolarized MRI

Co-Principal Investigator · $50,000 · September 2020 – March 2022

completed

Mechanistic modeling of tumor growth informed by hyperpolarized MRI, with David Fuentes, James Bankson and J. Tinsley Oden.

Held as co-PI under David Fuentes, and reported apart from the PI-led total. It is where the optimal-experimental-design line began: which measurement to make, given that the model is uncertain and the experiment is expensive. That question carried forward to mutual-information design for hyperpolarized ¹³C-pyruvate MRI and, more recently, to design for magnetic-nanoparticle hyperthermia.
